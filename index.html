Full Story with Audio & Easy Bilingual Notes

1 Part 1: The Search Begins — Sun God & Rain God (Page 118)

Once upon a time, there was a mother rat who wanted to get her young daughter married as soon as possible, to the most powerful being that she could find. ‘Who is the most powerful being on earth?’ she asked herself. She saw the bright sun god travelling across the sky, and thought, ‘Surely, all beings depend on the sun. The sun god is the most powerful being on this earth.’ She asked the sun god, ‘Are you the most powerful being on this earth?’ He smiled, ‘No, there is someone greater than me to help the creatures – it is the rain. Without the rain, no crop or tree would grow. There would be no water on earth.’

Just then, it began to rain. She thought, “How wonderful the rain is! It makes the whole land green. It makes the rivers flow. Surely, the rain god is the most powerful being on this earth.” She asked the rain god, ‘Are you the most powerful being on this earth?’ He smiled, ‘No, there is someone greater than me to help the creatures – it is the mountain. Without the mountain, there would be no protection for the creatures of this earth. The mountain blocks the clouds, and lets the water flow safely for the people and all life in the valleys.’

💡 Easy Summary: A mother rat wanted her young daughter to marry the most powerful being on earth so she would be safe. She asked the Sun God, who directed her to the Rain God (who gives water for crops). The Rain God then pointed her to the Mountain God (who blocks clouds and guides river water safely into valleys).
తెలుగు వివరణ: ఒకానొకప్పుడు ఒక తల్లి ఎలుక తన కూతురిని భూమి మీద అత్యంత శక్తివంతుడైన వాడికి ఇచ్చి పెళ్లి చేయాలనుకుంది. మొదట సూర్యభగవానుడిని అడిగితే, తనకంటే పంటలను పెంచే వర్షదేవుడే గొప్పవాడని చెప్తాడు. వర్షదేవుడిని అడిగితే, మేఘాలను ఆపి లోయల్లోకి నీటిని సురక్షితంగా పంపే పర్వతరాజే (కొండ) తనకంటే గొప్పవాడని చెప్తాడు.
2 Part 2: The Mountain God & The Humble Earthworm (Page 118)

She looked around, and saw the beautiful blue mountain. She thought, “How big and strong the mountain is! It withstands all winds and storms. It protects the earth and its creatures. Surely, the mountain god is the most powerful being on this earth.” She asked the mountain god, ‘Are you the most powerful being on this earth?’ He smiled, ‘No, there is someone greater than me to help the creatures – it is the worm. Without the worm, the earth would be hard and nothing would grow in the soil. The earthworm is the greatest friend that living beings can have.’

💡 Easy Summary: The Mother Rat admired the huge blue mountain that withstands winds and storms. But the Mountain God humbly said that the tiny Earthworm is greater because it loosens the hard soil so plants can grow, making it the greatest friend of all living beings!
తెలుగు వివరణ: తుఫానులను, గాలులను తట్టుకొని నిలబడే పర్వతరాజును తల్లి ఎలుక అడిగినప్పుడు, తనకంటే వానపాము (Earthworm) గొప్పదని చెప్తాడు. వానపాము లేకపోతే నేల గట్టిపడిపోయి ఏ మొక్కా మొలవదని, జీవులన్నింటికీ వానపామే గొప్ప స్నేహితుడని వివరిస్తాడు.
3 Part 3: The Daughter Rat’s Wise Questions (Page 119)

Just then, she saw her daughter coming towards her. She asked her mother, ‘What are you doing?’

‘I am trying to find out who the most powerful being on earth is,’ said the mother. ‘Why?’ asked her daughter. ‘I want you to marry him and be safe,’ said the mother.

‘Why would I need to marry to be safe?’ asked the daughter. ‘To be safe, I need to know how to take care of myself.’

‘You are small. You need protection,’ said the mother rat.

‘The best protection is to be able to protect oneself,’ said the daughter. ‘To protect myself, I need to learn to be strong and work hard.’

‘But why would you need to work? If you marry someone rich and powerful, he will support you,’ said the mother rat.

‘Who is rich and powerful, amma?’ asked the daughter. ‘The truly powerful being is one who can take care of oneself and those she loves. One is truly rich, if one is rich in love. I want to be powerful myself, so that I can take care of myself and those that I love.’

💡 Easy Summary: The daughter rat tells her mother that marriage is not a shortcut to safety. The best protection is learning to protect oneself by being strong and working hard. True power is taking care of oneself and loved ones, and true wealth is being rich in love!
తెలుగు వివరణ: సురక్షితంగా ఉండటానికి పెళ్లి చేసుకోవాల్సిన అవసరం లేదని, తనను తాను రక్షించుకోవడమే అత్యుత్తమ రక్షణ అని కూతురు ఎలుక తల్లికి చెప్తుంది. కష్టపడి పనిచేయడం, ధైర్యంగా ఉండటం నేర్చుకుంటానని, తనను తాను చూసుకుంటూ తాను ప్రేమించే వారిని కూడా చూసుకోగలగడమే నిజమైన శక్తి అని చెప్తుంది.
4 Part 4: Standing on One’s Own Feet — The Power Within (Page 119)

The mother rat was confused. ‘What will you do?’ she asked. ‘I will learn to stand on my own feet. I will find work to do that supports me, and my family. For that, I need to learn more about the world, and learn to live in it as a good creature. Let me first learn to take care of myself.’

‘But don’t you need help?’ asked the mother rat.

‘Yes, from you, amma!’ said the daughter. ‘Help me support myself. I am not interested in marrying anybody, rich or powerful. Depending on another person’s power, position or prosperity does not promise peace and security in the long run. One has to depend on the power within oneself to seek the target in one’s life.’

💡 Easy Summary: The daughter asks her mother for one real help: "Help me support myself!" She explains that depending on someone else's power, position, or wealth never guarantees lasting peace or security. Every girl must rely on the power within herself to achieve her life's goals.
తెలుగు వివరణ: తన సొంత కాళ్లపై తాను నిలబడటానికి సహాయం చేయమని కూతురు తల్లిని అడుగుతుంది. వేరొకరి అధికారం, పదవి లేదా సంపదపై ఆధారపడటం వల్ల శాశ్వతమైన శాంతి, భద్రత లభించవని, మన జీవిత లక్ష్యాన్ని చేరుకోవడానికి మనలోని అంతర్గత శక్తిపైనే ఆధారపడాలని కూతురు స్పష్టం చేస్తుంది.

Answer the following questions (Model Notes + Practice)

1. What do you think is the most important thing to learn to live well?

Model Answer: The most important thing to learn to live well is self-reliance (learning to take care of oneself and stand on one's own feet). Instead of depending on others' wealth or power, we must gain knowledge about the world, work hard, earn our own livelihood, and live with love, dignity, and confidence.

తెలుగులో: బాగా జీవించడానికి నేర్చుకోవాల్సిన అత్యంత ముఖ్యమైన విషయం స్వయం సమృద్ధి (మన సొంత కాళ్లపై మనం నిలబడటం). ఇతరులపై ఆధారపడకుండా విద్య, కష్టపడే తత్వంతో మనల్ని మనం పోషించుకోవడం నేర్చుకోవాలి.

2. What are the skills or qualities that would help you to be independent in your life?

Model Answer: The essential skills and qualities that help us become independent in life are:

  • Good Education & Knowledge about the world.
  • Hard Work & Determination to achieve our goals.
  • Self-Confidence & Courage to face problems without fear.
  • Vocational / Career Skills & Financial Literacy to earn a living.
  • Decision-Making & Communication Skills to live as a responsible citizen.

తెలుగులో: మంచి విద్య, కష్టపడే తత్వం, ఆత్మవిశ్వాసం, ధైర్యం, మరియు వృత్తి నైపుణ్యాలు మనల్ని జీవితంలో స్వతంత్రంగా నిలబడేలా చేస్తాయి.

3. Do you agree / disagree with the daughter of the mother rat? Give reasons for your response.

Model Answer: Yes, I strongly agree with the daughter rat for the following reasons:

  • Self-Protection is the Best Protection: No one can protect us forever unless we learn to protect ourselves.
  • External Wealth is Temporary: Depending on another person's power, position, or prosperity does not guarantee peace, respect, or security in the long run.
  • Empowerment: When a girl is educated and earns her own living, she can support both herself and her family with dignity.

తెలుగులో: అవును, నేను కూతురు ఎలుక అభిప్రాయంతో పూర్తిగా ఏకీభవిస్తాను. ఎందుకంటే ఇతరుల ఆస్తి, అధికారాలపై ఆధారపడితే శాశ్వత భద్రత ఉండదు. సొంత శక్తిపై ఆధారపడినప్పుడే ఆత్మగౌరవంతో జీవించగలం.

I. Answers to the 7 Bar Diagram Questions (Pages 120–121):

1. In which year is the difference in infant mortality rates between male and female the highest?

Ans: In the year 2005, the difference between female IMR (61) and male IMR (56) is the highest (a difference of 5 points).

2. In which case and in which year do we find a sudden decrease in the IMR?

Ans: In the year 2005 (between 2000 and 2005), we find a sudden, sharp decrease in both Male and Female IMR (Male IMR dropped by 11 points from 67 to 56, and Female IMR dropped by 8 points from 69 to 61).

3. What will happen if there is a wide gap in IMR between male and female?

Ans: If there is a wide gap in IMR between male and female, the number of girls in society will decrease sharply, leading to an imbalanced sex ratio and serious social problems.

4. What, according to you, may the reasons be for the female IMR being higher than the male IMR?

Ans: Gender discrimination, preference for male children, neglect of girl infants' nutrition and medical care, and superstitions viewing girls as a financial burden.

5. What may the reasons be for the decrease in IMR over the years?

Ans: Improved medical and healthcare facilities, pulse polio and vaccination drives, better maternal nutrition, institutional deliveries, and growing literacy and awareness among parents.

6. Do you think there could be a further decrease in the IMR after 5 years?

Ans: Yes, definitely. With advanced healthcare programs, 108/102 ambulance services, government nutrition schemes, and women's education, IMR will decrease further.

7. What, according to you, may the reasons be for the death of more than half of both male and female infants?

Ans: Malnutrition among pregnant mothers, premature births, lack of clean drinking water and sanitation, poverty, illiteracy, and lack of timely hospital care in remote rural areas.

Page 121 • Study Skills II (Group Work)

Analytical Report on the Infant Mortality Rates (IMR) in India

Analytical Report: Trends in Infant Mortality Rates (IMR) in India (1990–2008)

1. Introduction: The bar diagram provided by the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MOSPI) illustrates the Infant Mortality Rates (IMR) of male and female infants in India over an 18-year period from 1990 to 2008.

2. Key Observations & Data Trends: In 1990, the IMR was alarmingly high at 78 for males and 81 for females (80 overall). Over the years, there has been a steady decline: in 1995 (Male 73, Female 76) and in 2000 (Male 67, Female 69). A sudden and significant drop occurred between 2000 and 2005, where Male IMR fell to 56 and Female IMR to 61. By 2008, it further decreased to 52 for males and 55 for females.

3. Gender Disparity (Higher Female IMR): Throughout all the recorded years from 1990 to 2008, the female IMR has remained consistently higher than the male IMR, with the highest gap of 5 points recorded in 2005. This reflects deep-rooted gender bias, where girl infants often receive less nutrition and medical attention compared to boys.

4. Conclusion & Recommendations: While better vaccination, healthcare, and literacy have successfully reduced IMR from 80 (in 1990) to 53 (in 2008), the survival gap between boy and girl infants must be eliminated through strict awareness campaigns, maternal nutrition schemes, and equal care for the girl child.

Page 121 • Oral Activity A (Debate)

Debate: “Reservation in education, employment and legislature will empower the women.”

FOR the Motion (అనుకూలంగా వాదనలు)
  • Historical Justice: For centuries, women were denied equal access to schooling and jobs (like Akkayya in Bonsai Life). Reservation provides a fair starting line.
  • Financial Independence: Reservation in colleges and government jobs ensures women earn their own livelihood and live with dignity.
  • Voice in Law-Making (Legislature): When women have reserved seats in Parliament, Assemblies, and Panchayats, laws protecting women, children, health, and education are prioritized.
  • Role Models: Seeing women as doctors, officers, and leaders inspires rural parents to educate their daughters.
Counter-Perspective / Challenges (వ్యతిరేక / ప్రత్యామ్నాయ వాదనలు)
  • Grassroots Education First: Reservation at higher levels helps only if girls first receive quality primary and secondary schooling without dropping out.
  • Change in Mindset Needed: True empowerment requires changing patriarchal attitudes at home so women can make independent decisions, not just hold nominal posts.
  • Merit & Equal Capability: Women are equally intelligent and capable of excelling in any field on their own merit when given equal encouragement and safety.

B. Give Your Opinion on the 4 Statements & Discuss with Classmates

Vote Agree/Disagree on each statement and read the ready-to-speak classroom opinion!

1. “Interest in music, dance and sports distract from studies.”

Model Opinion (Disagree): I strongly disagree. Music, dance, and sports do not distract from studies; instead, they refresh our mind, reduce stress, build discipline, and promote all-round development. Many great achievers excel in both academics and arts/sports!

2. “One should not be punished for telling the truth.”

Model Opinion (Agree): I completely agree. Telling the truth requires honesty and courage. If children are punished when they admit a mistake truthfully, they may start lying out of fear. Instead, honesty should be appreciated and guided gently.

3. “When an older person says something, we should blindly obey.”

Model Opinion (Disagree): While we must always respect our elders and value their experience, we should not obey blindly. Just like the daughter rat who politely questioned her mother, we should think rationally, ask respectful questions, and never obey anything that is wrong or unsafe.

4. “It is wrong for actors to model for cigarettes and fairness creams.”

Model Opinion (Agree): I strongly agree. Youth and children look up to actors as role models. Promoting cigarettes harms public health and causes cancer, while fairness cream ads create a false inferiority complex based on skin colour. True beauty lies in character and health, not skin tone.

Personal Body Safety Rules (NO – GO – TELL)

1. Clothing Rules

We keep private parts covered in front of others. Though we don’t cover our mouth, it is private too.

2. Touching Rules

We don’t touch private parts in front of others, and no one should touch ours.

3. Talking Rules

We talk openly about safety and private parts with Safe Adults (parents/trusted teachers).

🚨 What to do if someone breaks Personal Body Safety Rules?

1. Say ‘NO’ firmly to that person  |  2. GO away from that person immediately  |  3. TELL a Safe Adult (or call Childline 1098) because that person is doing something unsafe and must be stopped.

Remember: Shame and blame are NEVER in the victim's body. Those who trouble children are the ones to be blamed and punished under the law (POCSO Act 2012).
